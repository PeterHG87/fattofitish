Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This app ("Fat to Fitish") is a personal fitness and weight-tracking tool. This policy explains what data is collected and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (handled entirely by Supabase's authentication system — we never see or store your password ourselves)
  • Information you enter: weight, height, age, sex, activity level, goals, food logs, step counts, and training sessions

How it's used

Your data is used only to power the app's own features for you — calculating targets, showing your history and charts. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond running the app itself.

Who can see it

Only you can see your personal logs, weight, and targets — this is enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app. The searchable food list (calories/macros for specific foods) is shared between accounts using this app, but your personal logs are not.

Data storage

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider. No data is stored on the device beyond what's needed for the app to function.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Edit goals → Delete my account). This cannot be undone.
